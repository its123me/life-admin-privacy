Life Admin Privacy Policy

```

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Overview

Life Admin is designed to help you organize important personal information such as deadlines, renewals, bills, subscriptions, appointments, documents, notes and reminders.

2. Data Storage

Life Admin is designed as a local-first application. Information that you enter into the app is stored locally on your device.

3. Personal Information

Life Admin does not require you to create an account in order to use the core application.

You should only enter information that you are comfortable storing on your device.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Life Admin may use your device's local notification functionality to display reminders that you have configured.

5. Data Sharing

Life Admin does not sell your personal information.

The app is designed so that your life-admin information remains on your device rather than being automatically transmitted to a remote server.

6. Third-Party Services

Life Admin does not currently require third-party advertising services or social-media tracking services for its core functionality.

7. Data Deletion

Because your information is stored locally, you can remove information from the app using the app's available deletion functions. Removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored application data, depending on your device settings and operating system.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Life Admin's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
```
